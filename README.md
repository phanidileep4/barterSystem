# BarterSystem

An open experiment in whether AI can make exchange without immediate money more practical, trustworthy, and useful.

## Why this repository exists

I started this repository in September 2019 with a simple intuition: direct exchange might become relevant again if technology could remove some of the friction that made traditional barter difficult.

The idea stayed dormant for seven years. I am reviving it now because AI changes what is technically possible. Modern systems can understand loosely described needs, discover complementary offers, coordinate more than two participants, and explain why a potential exchange may work.

This is not a prediction that money will disappear. Money remains an extraordinarily efficient medium of exchange. The question behind BarterSystem is narrower:

> Can technology help people exchange useful skills, time, goods, and spare capacity when money is unavailable, undesirable, or simply not the best expression of value?

## The concept

A participant can describe:

- what they can offer;
- what they need;
- where and when an exchange can happen;
- constraints, preferences, and boundaries;
- whether the exchange is local, remote, one-time, or recurring.

The system can then help discover:

- direct exchanges between two people;
- multi-party exchange paths when a direct match does not exist;
- skill-for-skill, time-for-time, good-for-service, and other combinations;
- potential matches written in ordinary language rather than rigid categories.

Every exchange remains voluntary. AI may suggest and explain; people decide.

## A possible first MVP

The first useful version should stay intentionally small:

1. Create a profile with offers and needs.
2. Browse and search active listings.
3. Receive explainable match suggestions.
4. Propose, counter, accept, decline, or cancel an exchange.
5. Record completion and leave structured feedback.
6. Report unsafe, deceptive, or inappropriate activity.

The initial goal is to test whether people find the matching model useful—not to build a new currency or a giant marketplace.

## Where AI may help

- **Semantic matching:** understand that “resume feedback” and “career coaching” may be related without requiring identical tags.
- **Multi-party matching:** identify exchange loops that solve the traditional need for two people to want exactly what the other offers.
- **Explainable suggestions:** show why a match was proposed and which constraints may conflict.
- **Coordination:** help participants clarify scope, timing, location, and expected outcomes.
- **Safety assistance:** flag suspicious patterns for human review without pretending automated judgment is infallible.

AI should not secretly price people, rank human worth, or make binding decisions.

## Principles

- Human choice over automated optimization.
- Explainable suggestions over mysterious scores.
- Privacy and data minimization from the beginning.
- Safety, consent, accessibility, and dispute handling as core product work.
- No speculative token or cryptocurrency required.
- No promise that barter replaces wages, public policy, or social safety nets.
- Compliance with applicable tax, labor, consumer-protection, and local laws.

## Project status

BarterSystem is currently in **discovery and design**. There is no production application yet, and the technical stack is deliberately undecided.

The immediate work is to pressure-test the problem, define a responsible exchange model, and agree on the smallest experiment worth building. See the [roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md) and the repository issues for concrete starting points.

## Contributing

Thoughtful contributions are welcome from engineers, designers, economists, community organizers, trust-and-safety practitioners, researchers, and people with experience in mutual-aid or exchange networks.

Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). You do not need to write code to contribute; research, criticism, workflows, threat models, and small prototypes are all valuable at this stage.

## Important note

Barter transactions can still create tax, legal, safety, and reporting obligations. This repository is an exploratory open-source project, not financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.
