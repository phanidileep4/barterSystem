# Code of Conduct

BarterSystem should be a respectful place to test ideas, disagree constructively, and learn in public.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.
- Critique ideas and implementations without attacking people.
- Make room for contributors who are new to open source or the subject matter.
- Be honest about evidence, uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and limitations.
- Protect personal information and obtain consent before sharing another person's story or data.
- Consider how proposals may affect people with less power, access, or technical knowledge.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam, fraud, or attempts to use the project to facilitate illegal or exploitative exchanges are not acceptable.

## Reporting

For ordinary project disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request. For sensitive conduct or safety concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the contact method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not post another person's private information in a public issue.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or participants when necessary to protect the community and the project.
